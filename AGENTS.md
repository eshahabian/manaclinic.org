# Mana Clinic product policy

- Until the user explicitly changes this policy, do not display service prices,
  tariffs, price ranges, discounts, or numeric fees on public pages, therapist
  profiles, service cards, articles, SEO metadata, or structured data.
- Keep existing authenticated appointment accounting and payment flows intact.
  Do not interpret SEO or conversion improvements as permission to publish prices.
- Publish only verified clinic credentials, staff roles, medical reviewers,
  article references, and real photos. Do not invent missing trust information.
